“Every cup starts with a choice.”

A barista simulation that goes from bean to cup, right in your browser. Pick a bean, dial in the grind, pull the shot, texture the milk and master the pour-over.

How to play
  • Espresso: slide the grinder from fine to coarse, then hold Pull (or press Space) to run the shot. Aim for about 36 g in the cup inside the bean’s time window. A fast shot means grind finer, a slow one means grind coarser. The shot keeps dripping for a moment after you stop.
  • Latte: pull the espresso, then steam the milk. Keep the wand tip near the surface early to stretch in air, then go deeper to roll and heat it. Stop around 60 °C with silky microfoam.
  • Pour-over: hold Heat to bring the kettle up to temperature, start the bloom pour, and wait for the right bloom time before the main pour. Water temperature and bloom time decide the score.
  • Keyboard: Space or Enter does the main action, the left and right arrows move the slider, 1 to 4 picks a bean, and Esc goes back to the menu. Mouse and touch work too.
  • Your progress, best scores and unlocks save in this browser.
