Privacy

Alien Builders collects no data. No accounts, no names, no ads, no tracking, no analytics and no internet needed to play.

Your stars, watches, drawings and saved builds are stored only on this device (in the app's local storage). They never leave the device. Deleting the app (or clearing the browser's site data) erases them.

Grown-ups: the full policy is on the Alien Builders App Store page and at rowbaylis8-cmd.github.io/browser-games/alien-builders-privacy.html
