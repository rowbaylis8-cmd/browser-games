Alien Builders - Privacy Policy

Effective 2 October 2026. Applies to the Alien Builders iPhone/iPad app and the web version at rowbaylis8-cmd.github.io/browser-games/alien-builders/.
In short: Alien Builders does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics, no tracking and no chat. The game does not need the internet to play.

What the game saves

To remember your progress, the game saves your stars, unlocked levels, collected Morphlink watches, your Drawing Pad picture, your saved builds and your settings (sound on/off, chosen alien and watch). These are stored only on your own device, in the app's local storage (or your browser's local storage for the web version). They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

You can erase everything at any time by deleting the app, or for the web version by clearing the site data for this website in your browser settings.

What we do not collect

We do not collect names, email addresses, ages, photos, voice, contacts, location, device identifiers, advertising identifiers or usage statistics. The app asks for no device permissions (no camera, microphone, photos, location or notifications). The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking code.

Children

Alien Builders is made for children. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it does not collect personal information from children, consistent with the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws. There are no links to social media and no way to talk to other players.

The web version and hosting

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may keep standard technical server logs (such as IP addresses) when the page is downloaded; see GitHub's privacy statement. The game itself sends nothing anywhere. Once installed to the Home Screen it works offline.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. We will not start collecting personal information without updating this policy first.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Please use the developer contact details shown on the Alien Builders App Store page.
