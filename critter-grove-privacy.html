Critter Grove — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 October 2026

Developer: Rowallen Baylis · Contact: rowallenbaylis92@gmail.com

Critter Grove ("the app") is a creature-collecting map game. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Location

If you allow it, the app uses your device's location (precise or approximate) only on your device to move your character on the in-game map. Your location is never sent to us or to anyone else, is not stored on any server, and is not shared. You can refuse or revoke location permission at any time in your device settings; the game then uses demo movement.

Data we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers of its own. Your game progress is saved only in the app's local storage on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Data sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer any personal data to third parties.

In-app purchases

Optional purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or receive your payment details. Google's handling of purchase information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
