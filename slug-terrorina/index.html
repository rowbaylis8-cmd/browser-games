Slug Terrorina

Queen of the Garden Rampage
How to play
You are Terrorina, a cartoon villain slug on a veggie-patch rampage!
• Move with Arrow keys or WASD (or the on-screen stick).
• Press Space or Jump to hop over salt piles and salt throws.
• Chomp lettuce, tomatoes, carrots and berries to grow and score.
• Slither close to bugs to scare them for bonus points.
• Dodge salt-flinging gardeners and swooping birds — it gets trickier as you go!
• Press M to mute sound.

Or press Enter / Space to start
