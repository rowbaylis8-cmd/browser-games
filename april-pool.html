🌸 April Pool 🌸

Spring 8-ball! Drag back from the cue ball to aim and set power, then let go to shoot. Pot all your Gold balls (1-7) or Diamonds (9-15), then the 8-ball. Pot the cue ball and your opponent gets a free spot. Miss, and the pocket skeletons will come up to giggle at you!
