Pocket Pixel Arcade - Privacy Policy

Effective 10 October 2026. Applies to the Pocket Pixel Arcade Android app (com.rowbaylis.arcade) and the games in the Browser Games Arcade at rowbaylis8-cmd.github.io/browser-games/.
In short: Pocket Pixel Arcade does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics, no tracking and no chat. All games are inside the app and play without the internet.

What the games save

Some games remember your progress, such as stars, unlocked levels, levels you made, builds, scores and settings like sound on/off. These are saved only on your own device, in the app's local storage. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

You can erase everything at any time by deleting the app (or clearing its storage in Android Settings).

What we do not collect

We do not collect names, email addresses, ages, photos, voice, contacts, location, device identifiers, advertising identifiers or usage statistics. The app asks for no special device permissions (no camera, microphone, photos, location or notifications). It contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking code.

Purchases

The app is a one-time paid download sold through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google; we never see your payment details. See Google's privacy policy for how Google handles purchases.

Children

Pocket Pixel Arcade is made for children and families. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it does not collect personal information from children, consistent with the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), Google Play's Families policy and similar laws. There are no links to social media and no way to talk to other players.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. We will not start collecting personal information without updating this policy first.
