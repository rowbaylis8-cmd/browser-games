MORPHLINK

  
SURVIVAL

  
Kid inventor Milo Rivers found a strange alien wristwatch called the Morphlink. Morph into wild new aliens, gather, build, and survive the monster-filled nights!

  
  
  
  

  

  






  
How to Play

  
Keyboard & Mouse

  

    
WASD / Arrows Move

    
Click / Space Attack & gather

    
Right-click / Shift Special power

    
F Morph (or turn back)

    
Q / E / 1-6 / Tab Alien wheel

    
B Build & craft menu

    
P / Esc Pause

    
M Sound on/off

  

  
Touch (tablet / phone)

  

    
Left side drag Joystick move

    
Big button Attack (auto-aims)

    
Star button Special power

    
Watch button Open alien wheel

    
Hammer button Build menu

    
Tap an alien slot Quick morph

  

  
Tips

  
    
    
  • Punch trees, rocks and glowing crystals to collect wood, stone and energy crystals.
    • 
    
  • The Morphlink only lasts a little while, then it needs to recharge. Use your alien time wisely!
    • 
    
  • Nights are dark and full of tougher monsters. Build campfires to heal and walls to hide behind.
    • 
    
  • Every dawn you unlock a new alien. Spend crystals to unlock aliens early or upgrade your watch.
    • 
    
  • Beat 10 levels of alien invaders! Zap enough aliens to clear each level. Level 5 ends with a mini-boss, and on Level 10 the giant King Grumblor shows up. Good luck, hero!
    • 
    
  • Every enemy alien type has its own colors and pattern (spots, stripes, checkers, glow...). A NEW ALIEN! card tells you what each new one does.
    • 
    
  • Your progress is saved: pick any level you have reached from the Levels menu.
    •
