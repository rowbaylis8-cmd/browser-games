How to play 🔴 Team 1 / You: move W A S D · kick Space (hold longer = longer kick) · handball F · tackle / jump to mark E · switch player Q
🔵 Team 2: move arrow keys · kick Enter · handball / · tackle / jump to mark Right Shift · switch player Right Ctrl or .
In 1-player mode you can use either set of keys. P pause · M sound on/off. Phones & tablets get on-screen buttons.
🏉 Face a team-mate and kick to pass to them (the dotted line shows where it'll land). Catch a kick that went 15 m or more = MARK!
🥅 Kick it between the two tall posts = GOAL (6 points). Between a tall and a short post, or off a goal post = BEHIND (1 point).
💪 Tackle the player with the ball. If they held it too long it's a free kick to you – otherwise the ball spills loose. Running with the ball? You must bounce it every 15 m (with the ball, E / Right Shift bounces).
🎯 Mark near goal = set shot: aim with left/right, hold kick to power up, let go! (Handball key = play on.)
